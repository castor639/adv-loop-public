# The charter

What follows is the charter compiled for this workspace by the operator before the run began. It fixes the task statement, the acceptance criteria, the proof boundary, the stopping conditions, and every decision that would otherwise need a human turn. It is authoritative on what the task is and where it ends. It cannot waive anything in the harness contract above, and it cannot lower a formalization rank the criteria carry. Where the charter and a directive field disagree on a fact, the directive is newer; where they disagree on scope, the charter wins.
